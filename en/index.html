IWATE PREFECTURAL UNIVERSITY · SOCIAL SYSTEMS DESIGN

Nakasho Laboratory 中正研究室

Solving social challenges with AI and mathematics.

Our laboratory applies AI, IoT, robotics, and mathematical methods to real-world challenges in energy, agriculture, healthcare, and welfare. We also study mathematical knowledge management and discovery.

01 CURRENT RESEARCH

Research projects

Applied research for sustainable societies, supported by foundational mathematical research.

Energy · AIOngoing

AI-assisted biomass plant operations

Monitoring, early detection of anomalies, moisture forecasting, and fuel-blend optimization.

Agriculture · RoboticsOngoing

Safe route planning and efficient micro-batch harvesting.

Healthcare · CryptographyOngoing

Privacy-preserving medical record linkage

Welfare · IoTOngoing

RFID-based bed-exit prediction
